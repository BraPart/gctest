Who we are

Our Company

Experts Corner General Contracting Co. (ECGCC) story started about 10 years ago in 2014 in a small office in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia as a local establishment focusing on small construction projects. During this journey, ECGCC has taken huge leaps to become a top tier Grade A general contracting company offering the full spectrum of integrated and turnkey engineering, procurement and construction services and successfully delivering increasingly larger and more complex projects for many public sector, private and Public-Private Partnership clients not only in Saudi Arabia but also in the UAE and Qatar.

Who we are

Health, Safety & Environmental (HSE) Policy

Experts Corner General Contracting Co. is strongly and actively committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace and protecting the environment and communities that could be affected by our operations and project execution. We recognize that developing and engraining a responsible and robust health, safety and environmental program into our business are key to our success.

The safety and wellbeing of employees, customers, subcontractors, suppliers and the communities we work and live in are paramount. No one will be expected, directed or encouraged to expose themselves or others to any uncontrolled risk in order to satisfy a work-related requirement.

We comply with all pertinent environmental and safety laws, rules and regulations, not just because it is legally or contractually required, but also because we believe it is the responsible way to conduct our business.

At Experts Corner General Contracting Co. we believe that all work-related accidents, incidents, injuries, and illnesses are preventable. We therefore strive to achieve a Zero Incidents Goal by:

  • Increasing awareness of each employee’s responsibility to identify and eliminate unsafe practices and conditions in the workplace;
  • Instilling a safety team mentality so all employees contribute to the efforts, and all manager and supervisors are aware of their team’s capabilities are genuinely accountable for the team’s actions; and
  • Creating a culture in which everyone accepts responsibility and accountability for his or her own safety and health as well as the safety and health of coworkers, and feels empowered to promptly report noncompliance or unsafe conditions and to take immediate action to prevent injuries or environmental accidents.

Experts Corner General Contracting Co. requires all employees to embrace these values and play their parts in creating and maintaining a 100% safe culture. Executive and line management has an obligation to lead by example and play an active role in the communication and implementation of, and ensuring compliance with, our HSE policies, procedures and standards.

This policy will be continually reviewed and enhanced to keep them current and relevant as part of Experts Corner General Contracting Co. strategic Commitment to Continuous Improvement of the processes and procedures of our business.

Who we are

Quality Policy

We at Experts Corner General Contracting Co. pride ourselves in our people, our processes, and the materials and services we deliver to our customers. We are passionate about excellence and doing our work right the first time.

We believe in achieving quality by making informed decisions based on subject matter expertise and facts, identifying issues before they occur, correcting non-conformances promptly when they are identified, and motivating every person in the company to “do it right the first time.”

We therefore focus on behaviors that deliver “right the first time” quality, or Quality Imperatives. Our Quality Imperatives serve as an internal compass to guide all ECGCC employees in delivering their quality mandate across our business, on every project, in all functions, and at every level of our organization. They also drive the selection of our project delivery partners, including subcontractors, suppliers and consultants.

We provide the processes, tools, training, and expertise to engrain the early identification and timely resolution of quality issues while capturing lessons learned and best practices.

Experts Corner General Contracting Co. is diligently committed to a quality management system that complies with the International Standard ISO 9001:2015. Our Quality Management System provides our customers with the assurance that the projects completed by ECGCC deliver a level of quality that consistently conforms to contract and statutory requirements.

Our Quality Management System and Quality Policy will be continually reviewed and enhanced to keep them current and relevant as part of Experts Corner General Contracting Co. strategic Commitment to Continuous Improvement of the processes and procedures of our business.

CEO message

It is with immense pride and gratitude that I reflect on the remarkable journey of Experts Corner General Contracting Co. (ECGCC) over the past decade. From our modest beginnings in 2014, operating out of a small office in Riyadh, we have evolved into a top-tier Grade A general contracting company, renowned for our comprehensive suite of engineering, procurement, and construction services.

Our growth has been driven by our unwavering commitment to excellence and innovation. What began as a local establishment focusing on small-scale projects has expanded to encompass an extensive portfolio of increasingly complex and large-scale projects. Our success in delivering high-quality outcomes for public sector, private, and Public-Private Partnership clients across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Qatar is a testament to the hard work, dedication, and expertise of our exceptional team.

At ECGCC, we take pride in our ability to provide integrated and turnkey solutions that meet the diverse needs of our clients. Our approach is centered on understanding and anticipating client requirements, ensuring that each project is executed with precision, efficiency, and a relentless focus on quality.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to upholding the values that have brought us this far—integrity, excellence, and innovation. We are excited about the opportunities that lie ahead and look forward to continuing to build lasting partnerships and delivering exceptional results.
